Heavy fruit set on the tomatoes, still all green and a few weeks from color.
The copper cages carry the weight as the trusses fill. Basil shares the bed as
a companion, and the deep pine-straw mulch holds moisture and keeps soil from
splashing up onto the lower leaves, which is where blight tends to start.
