Romas are the workhorse of the tomato bed, and this basket is a single
harvest. Their meaty, low-water flesh is what makes them the right choice for
canning: less time reducing on the stove, more usable pulp per tomato.

This basket, along with the ones that follow it, is what feeds the canning
sessions running through the back half of July.
