A full week of canning, running from July 15 through July 21. The Roma harvests
from earlier in the month went in whole and into salsa, and the banana pepper
surplus went into the pickling jars. It takes a full week of steady picking to
build up enough of any one crop to justify a canning batch, so this shelf is
several harvests' worth of tomatoes and peppers condensed down.

The payoff comes in winter, when this row of jars is the difference between
storebought and homegrown on the table.
