The banana pepper plant is carrying more fruit than it has all season, pale
yellow-green pods stacked from the lower branches to the top. Marigolds planted
as a companion have made a real difference here: pest pressure that used to
thin this bed has dropped off dramatically since we started interplanting them.

Worth repeating next year. The marigolds cost almost nothing in bed space and
the payoff on pest deterrence has been the clearest win of the summer rotation.
