Yellow pear tomatoes have been one of the most reliable producers in the bed,
and this tub is a single picking. They are small enough to eat straight off
the vine, but there are enough of them now that most go into the kitchen for
salads and snacking rather than getting eaten in the garden.

The plant shows no sign of slowing down, so this is likely one of several tubs
before the season is out.
