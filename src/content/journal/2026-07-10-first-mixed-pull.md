The first pull where everything ripened together. Romas make up most of the
basket, with a few yellow pear and cherry tomatoes and a handful of banana
peppers mixed in. This is the sign that peak summer harvest has arrived: once
one crop starts coming in daily, the rest tend to follow within days.

From here it is a matter of keeping up, picking often enough that nothing goes
past its window on the vine.
