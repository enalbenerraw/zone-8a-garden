Poblanos and banana peppers are ripening on the same schedule this year, so
they are coming in together. The poblanos add heat and size to the mix, while
the banana peppers keep producing steadily, helped along by the marigolds
planted nearby as a pest deterrent.

Between the two, there is enough here for roasting and for pickling, which is
exactly the kind of variety the pepper bed was planned for.
