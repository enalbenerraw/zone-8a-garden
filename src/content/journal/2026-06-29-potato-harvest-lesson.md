This is the entire potato harvest this year, and it is a teaching crop. The seed
potatoes went into the ground after the last frost on March 27, but in a bed that
was new for 2026. For context, in 2025 a different bed gave us nearly 45 pounds of
red and yellow potatoes, so this is well short of that.

Two corrections explain the gap. We did not work beneficial nematodes into the new
bed's soil, and we did not net the plants as they grew, so Colorado potato beetles
overran the row. The fixes are known for next year: amend the soil with nematodes,
net early, and bring this bed into the normal rotation in 2027.
