Two more 4x8 raised beds went in for the cool season, filled straight from the
back of the truck. In October they were planted with:

- **Garlic**
- **Broccoli**
- **Spinach**
- **Lettuce**

The garlic is inside its window: extension planting dates for this region run
from mid-October through November. The rest went in later than the
[planting calendar](../../guides/planting-calendar/) suggests, which has broccoli
transplanted by mid-August and lettuce and spinach sown in September. These beds
are a real-world test of how much a late start still produces before the cold
sets in, and the [winter protection guide](../../guides/winter-protection/) is the
plan for getting them through it.
