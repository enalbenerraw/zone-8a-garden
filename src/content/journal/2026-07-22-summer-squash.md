Summer squash is still coming steadily this far into July, which is exactly
what the corn-and-squash pairing was meant to deliver. Block-planting corn
with squash sprawling underneath as a living mulch, first noted back in the
[corn tasseling entry](../2026-06-18-corn-and-squash/), has paid off on both
sides: the corn stayed shaded at the root and the squash has kept fruiting
long after a solo planting would usually start to fade.

Two more squash today, with the vines showing no sign of stopping yet.
