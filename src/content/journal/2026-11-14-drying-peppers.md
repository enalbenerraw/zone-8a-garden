This is the first year drying peppers. After the jars and the freezer bags, the
last of the ripe red peppers went up on a line indoors to dry by their stems.
The ones on the ends are already darkening and wrinkling; the plumper ones in
the middle have further to go.

Drying adds a third way to keep the pepper harvest alongside canning and
freezing, and it takes no jars and no freezer space.
