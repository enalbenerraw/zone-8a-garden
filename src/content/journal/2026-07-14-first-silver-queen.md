The first ears of the season, and the corn in every entry this year is Silver
Queen. Block-planting it with summer squash sprawling underneath, as noted back
in the [corn tasseling entry](../2026-06-18-corn-and-squash/), turned out to be
a real success: the squash covered the ground as a living mulch while the corn
had all summer to bulk up overhead.

Two ears is a small start, but the rest of the block is right behind it.
