The canning season closed out at **60 jars**, put up from the garden's
tomatoes, peppers, and cucumbers. That is a lot of jars for a household of
empty nesters, and it came from steady picking all summer rather than one big
harvest: each batch on this shelf is a few days of the same crop saved up
until there was enough to fill a canner.

The peppers that did not go into jars went into the freezer:

- **About 6 bags of poblanos**
- **About 4 bags of green peppers**

Between the shelf and the freezer, the summer beds will be feeding the kitchen
well into winter.
