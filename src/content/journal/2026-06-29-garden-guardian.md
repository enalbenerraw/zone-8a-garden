Not every entry is a crop. Our Golden Retriever loves to roam the beds with us,
and she has appointed herself guardian of the garden gate. Daylilies are blooming
behind her and a bean vine is climbing the gate, a good reminder that the garden
is as much a place to spend time as it is a place to grow food.
