The asparagus is past harvest and into its summer fern, which is exactly what
you want: the ferns feed the crowns for next spring. The wildflower strip
(zinnias, coreopsis) and dill pull pollinators and beneficial insects to this
end of the garden.

The one correction for next year is the fennel. It crept into this strip and,
per the [fennel isolation note](../../guides/bed-layout/) in the bed-layout
guide, it crosses with dill and is a poor neighbor. It moves to its own bed in
2027.
