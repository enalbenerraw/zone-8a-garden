---
title: "Year-round planting calendar"
date: 2026-06-28
---

The [planting calendar](../../guides/planting-calendar/) now covers all twelve
months, spring greens and peas through summer fruiting crops to the winter
brassicas. Every date is anchored to two frost dates (last spring frost around
March 27, first fall frost around November 10), so you can shift the whole
calendar to your own frost dates and keep the crop spacing intact. The
at-a-glance timeline spans the full year to match.
