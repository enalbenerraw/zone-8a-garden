---
title: "New guide: winter protection"
date: 2026-11-14
---

The new [winter protection guide](../../guides/winter-protection/) covers how much
cold each winter crop can take, which row cover weight to use, a freeze-night
checklist, what to harvest ahead of a hard freeze, carrying spinach through to
spring, and cover crops or mulch for beds that are already empty. Every number
comes from a university extension publication, with ranges shown where sources
disagree.
