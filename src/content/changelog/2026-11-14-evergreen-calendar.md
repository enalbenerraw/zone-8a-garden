---
title: "Fall calendar dates no longer tied to one year"
date: 2026-11-14
---

The fall and winter table in the [planting calendar](../../guides/planting-calendar/)
now gives month and day ("June 27", "August 8") instead of 2026 dates, to match
the spring and summer tables. The schedule itself is unchanged and still keyed to
a November 10 first frost, so it reads the same in any year.
