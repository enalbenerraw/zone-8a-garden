Dates below are built around a **target first-frost date of November 10** and a sequential-succession bed. Shift them earlier or later to match your own frost date, but hold the relative spacing: the seed-start and transplant dates are staged so the bed never sits empty and never asks one crop to wait on another.

## How to read it

- **Start indoors** crops (the brassicas and fennel) need a head start so they can be transplanted as stocky seedlings into the August heat. Start them under cover and keep them watered.
- **Direct-sow** crops (lettuces, spinach, arugula) go straight into the bed in September once the worst heat breaks. They germinate fast in cooling soil.
- The **harvest windows** overlap on purpose. A bed planned this way produces something to cut every week from October onward, and the brassicas and kale carry the hardest part of winter.
